Hello, and welcome. 👋

I've been meaning to do this for a while.

For as long as I can remember, I've had thoughts that needed somewhere to go — half-finished ideas, things I figured out and immediately forgot how I figured out, observations about the world that felt important at 11pm and probably still are. For a while those lived in scattered text files, the Notes app, the back of my head. This blog is my attempt to give them a real home.

## A little about me

I'm a software developer by trade, and I spend a lot of my time thinking about AI — not just as a tool, but as something that's genuinely reshaping what it means to build things, to work, and to be useful. I care about that deeply. I want technology to make life better in ways that actually matter, and I try to stay close to that question in the work I do every day.

Outside of that, I'm a dad to twin boys and a husband to my wife Katie. My family is everything to me — the anchor and the reason behind most of what I do.

I also think a lot. Maybe too much. I've been told I overthink things, and I won't argue with that. But I'd rather be the kind of person who wrestles with hard questions than one who doesn't notice them. I'm interested in philosophy, in agency, in what it means to live with intention. I believe people have more power to shape their circumstances than they usually give themselves credit for.

## What I'll write about

Whatever's on my mind, honestly. That might look like:

- Things I'm building and lessons learned the hard way
- AI — where it's going, what it means, what excites and concerns me
- Philosophy, ideas, and the kind of questions that don't have clean answers
- Reflections on being a dad, a husband, a person trying to do good work in the world

Some posts will be short — a thought I needed to get out. Others might be longer. No fixed cadence, no content calendar. Just whenever something is worth writing down.

Think of this as my public notebook. A place to scribble.

Thanks for being here. More soon.
